### Independent check machinery (new)

- **Molecular graphs from SVG geometry** (`_c24_svg_mol`):
  - parallel lines with offsets of 0.8–7 px and overlapping projections are clustered into one bond, whose order is the cluster size;
  - bond end-points are clustered into vertices, and an unlabeled vertex is an implicit carbon with H = 4 − bond orders;
  - formula labels (OH, COOH, NO₂/O₂N, OCH₃, CH₃/H₃C, O) attach to the vertex within 6 px of their box and expand from templates;
  - carbon/oxygen valence is asserted (charge-separated nitro O allowed).
- **Other structure sources:** condensed formulae (`_c24_condensed`, which handles `-`, `=`, `\equiv`, COO/COOH) and IUPAC names (`_c24_from_name`).
- **Stereo:** canonical branch codes find stereocentres. Burnside orbits under brute-force graph automorphisms count stereoisomers and detect meso forms.
- **Stoichiometry:** a SymPy nullspace gives the smallest integer coefficients for each reaction. VSEPR counting uses steric number = ½(V + Σ ligand contributions − charge). MO bond order comes from aufbau over σ/π levels.
- **Circuits** (`_c24_circuit`, `_c24_mna`):
  - `_netlist` union-find over wires;
  - cell polarity from plate path lengths, diode cathode from the triangle apex next to the bar, current-source direction from the arrowhead tip;
  - component values from the nearest printed label (a split "1 / kΩ" label is joined);
  - modified nodal analysis in SymPy (cells, shorted diodes, current sources, ideal op-amp $V_+ = V_-$);
  - diode-state enumeration with consistency tests;
  - the gate truth table is evaluated over all 8 inputs after asserting that each gate outline matches its type (XOR = 2 paths with no bubble, XNOR = 2 paths + bubble, NAND = arc + bubble).

## Notable findings

1. **The old 2024 FEEE figures were fabricated.**
   - Q94 was a "Power supply" box instead of a Y network of cells.
   - Q96 was three labelled circles ("AC circuit") instead of a two-diode circuit.
   - Q97 was a PNP transistor instead of an op-amp current-to-voltage stage.
   - Q98 was an op-amp triangle instead of an XNOR/XOR/NAND logic network.

   All four were replaced by booklet-faithful redraws.
2. **The old Chemistry options were partly fabricated.** Q79 (HgCl₂, Hg₂Cl₂, CH₃COOH) and Q87 (NH₃, H₂O, BeCl₂) did not match the booklet. The old data paired key C with CH₃COOH; in the booklet, option C is the terminal alkyne. Q80 had an invented fourth arrow.
3. **Q96 polarity matters.** The 600-DPI crop shows the 15 V cell with its **long plate at the bottom**, so the right-hand node is at −15 V and D2 is reverse-biased by 25 V. This gives a clean unique state: D1 on, V$_o$ = 5 V. If that cell were drawn the other way round, D2 would conduct, X would be 10 V from either side, and D1 would sit exactly at the conduction boundary. The verifier then finds two consistent states, both still 5 V, and flags the ambiguity.
4. **Q94 polarity** (also read at 600 DPI): the 4 V cell has + towards V₁, both 8 V cells have + towards their junctions, and E has + on top. Only with this reading does the key C (14, 2, 0) follow, and the netlist solve confirms it.
5. **Interpretation notes** (logged; official key kept):
   - **Q93:** with identical windings, a series capacitor in either winding produces the phase split. B follows the convention that the main winding stays directly across the supply.
   - **Q100:** 80 N m assumes constant developed power ($T \propto 1/n$). Under constant flux and armature current the answer would be 120 N m.
6. **Q73:** "most probable radius" for the 1s orbital equals $a_0/Z$, i.e. 26.45 pm, which the booklet prints as 26.5. Option A (0.0265) is the same number in nm.
