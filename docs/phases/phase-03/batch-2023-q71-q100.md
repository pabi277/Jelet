## Findings

1. **The old OCR was wrong in 26 of 30 items.** In four of them (Q84, Q92, Q95, Q75) the corrupted text made the official key look wrong or unprovable. Q84's old compound has 2 tertiary carbons, Q92's options had no correct pair, Q95's option A was 4 μF instead of 3/4 μF, and Q75 asked about d_z² (for which "45° from Z" is wrong). The booklet text agrees with the official key in every one.
2. **Q94 withdrawn:** the circuit as drawn gives V_AB = 1800/77 ≈ 23.4 V. Option (C) 38.3 V equals 1800/47, the Millman result **with the 2 Ω resistor removed**, which is most likely the setter's error. Reading the dot-less crossings as open gives 13.3 V, which also matches no option. The redraw keeps the dot-less crossings exactly as printed, and the check evaluates both readings.
3. **Q88 withdrawn:** (A) is physically true (ΔV_fus < 0, so dT/dP < 0). (D) "pressure can shorten the time to reach equilibrium" cannot be decided from thermodynamics, and the stem asks for "the correct statement" in a multiple-correct section. The answer set is ambiguous between {A} and {A, D}. The solution explains this and states **+2 marks (+24 twelfths) to all**.
4. **Q96 key dispute (official A kept):** "increases" requires constant core flux. With the usual fixed supply voltage, the ideal secondary voltage is frequency-independent (C). This is logged under `KEY_DISPUTES_2023[96]`.
5. **Interpretation notes (official keys kept):** Q87 (the printed "n + 1 ≤ 4" is read as n + l; both readings give {B, C}), Q98 (constant output power), Q99 (constant armature current), Q100 (the printed "100π" without t is read as 100πt; the power does not depend on phase).
6. **Withdrawn 2023 items:** Q6, Q46, Q88 and Q94, giving Cat-I +1 (+12 twelfths) and Cat-II +2 (+24 twelfths) to every candidate.
7. Figures: 99 SVGs in total, 0 orphans, 0 raw hex. 2023 stem figures are exactly {42, 48, 56, 60, 61, 66, 68, 70, 84, 91, 92, 94, 95}, with no option figures. The Q84 subscript collision seen in pymupdf previews was fixed by start-anchoring the label text at a computed left edge.
