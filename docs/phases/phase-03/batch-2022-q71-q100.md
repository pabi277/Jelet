| **Q82** | **Key B (4), with a note.** "How many isomers … C₄H₉Cl" is read as constitutional isomers. The structure-graph enumeration in `_c22_q82` (every labelled C₄ tree × every Cl position, up to isomorphism) finds exactly 4. 2-Chlorobutane has one stereocentre, so counting both enantiomers gives 5 (option D). That count is logged, not keyed. |

A completely unloaded rotor could instead creep towards the dangerous speed V/(kφᵣ); that reading matches no option. |

### How each item is checked

- **Chemistry:**
  - SymPy stoichiometry (Q71 mole ratio; Q72 molality from density).
  - de Broglie algebra (Q73).
  - An aufbau engine with Cr/Cu exceptions and ionisation from the highest n (Q74).
  - Conductivity definitions (Q75).
  - The Ostwald dilution law and its weak-acid limit (Q76).
  - One-electron species (Q77).
  - Amphoteric and ammine-complex tables (Q78).
  - Formula derivation, S replacing O (Q79).
  - Cl oxidation states versus acidity order (Q80).
  - VSEPR via `_c24_vsepr` (Q81).
  - Graph enumeration plus stereocentres (Q82).
  - ΔU = q + w (Q84).
  - `_c24_balance` for 6Li + N₂ → 2Li₃N with cation size (Q85).
  - Cation z/r and the strong-base parent (Q86).
  - An oxidation-number solver that also handles hydridic H in B₂H₆ (Q87).
  - Sign analysis of ΔG = ΔH − TΔS (Q88).
  - The sign of dK/dT from van 't Hoff (Q89).
  - Bond ΔEN (Q90).
- **FEEE, text items:**
  - RMS integral (Q91).
  - Complex referral Z₁ + a²Z₂ (Q92).
  - Phase lead to R and X (Q94).
  - Series-bulb power split (Q95).
- **FEEE, figure items (rebuilt from the SVG):**
  - **Q93:** the N-axis crossing (s = 0) is read from the curve. Each dot must lie on the polyline, and its slip is s = 1 − N/N_s (W = 1.2, X = 0.58, Y = 0.14, Z = −0.03).
  - **Q96:** netlist → MNA over both diode states for v_s = ±1. Only one state is consistent per half-cycle, which gives v₀ = 0 or v_s, so the average is −V_m/π.
  - **Q97:** the cell polarity comes from plate lengths: + is on the inverting input, so v₋ = +3 V. Then `solveset` of 6 sin θ > 3 gives (π/6, 5π/6) = 120°.
  - **Q98:** union-find over the 7-switch graph for **all 128 states**, compared with every option's Boolean expression.
  - **Q99:** cell polarities, the I_C arrow direction and the V_CB markings are read from the SVG, then KVL gives V_CC = I_C·R + V_CB = 4.5 V.
