## Findings

- **Q81, 1993 vs 2013 IUPAC naming.** The official key (A) "3-(2-Methylpropyl) hept-1-en-4-yne" follows the 1993 rule: the longest chain that contains *both* multiple bonds. Under the 2013 recommendations (P-44.1, longest chain first), the parent is the 8-carbon chain, giving **5-ethenyl-7-methyloct-3-yne**. That matches no option exactly: (C) "7-Methyl-5-ethenyl-oct-3-yne" has the right locants but the substituents are not in alphabetical order. The key A is kept, and the derivation records both names. The verifier computes both names from the SVG graph.
- **Q88, carbocation rearrangement.** The 2° cation at C2 gives **I** directly (Markovnikov). The 1,2-methyl shift to the 3° cation gives **II**. III (1-bromo-3,3-dimethylbutane, the anti-Markovnikov/peroxide product) and IV (1-bromo-2,3-dimethylbutane, Br on a primary carbon of the rearranged skeleton, which would need a primary cation) do not form under ionic HBr addition. The key is {A, B, C}, since "I and II" (C) is also true. The drawn I–IV are pinned to hand-encoded graphs, so any swapped label fails Q88.
- **Q95, netlist.** The condition for equal phase is $\omega L_1/R_1=\omega L_2/R_2$, i.e. (B) $R_1/R_2=L_1/L_2$. (A) is dimensionally inconsistent (Ω² vs H²). (C) is consistent but not the equal-time-constant condition. The counter-example $R_1=1, L_1=2, R_2=2, L_2=1$ satisfies both A and C, yet the phases differ.
- **Q100, NAND netlist.** Both input NANDs receive (A, B). Reading the A riser as a hop over the B wire is essential: if it were wired as a T-junction (A = B), the truth table would collapse.
- **Q98, caught by the coverage gate.** The first draft of the Q98 derivation had only 2 steps, and the new coverage gate flagged it as a stub. A virtual-short step ($v^-=v^+=v_{in}$, so $v_o=(1+R_f/R_1)v_{in}$) was added.
- **Gap found by tamper testing and closed.** Before this record was finalised, attaching a spoiler `stemFigure` to a no-diagram item (Q76/Q91/Q97) was *not* detected, because the Batch 4 checks lacked the `_figures_ok` assertion that Batch 3 had. It is now enforced for all 25 items.
- **Q75 polish:** `\rangle` was replaced by `>`.
